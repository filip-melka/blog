Weekly ("the app") is a habit tracker for iPhone. This policy explains what data the app collects. The short version: none.

## No data collection

Weekly does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically:

- **No accounts.** There is no sign-up, login, or user profile.
- **No servers.** Weekly has no backend. It does not send any data over the network.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The app does not use any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.
- **No third parties.** Weekly does not integrate with any third-party SDKs that collect data.

## Where your data lives

All habits, time logs, and settings you create in Weekly are stored locally on your device, using Apple's on-device storage. This data:

- Never leaves your device.
- Is not backed up to any service operated by us.
- May be included in your device's iCloud or iTunes backups, which are managed entirely by Apple and governed by [Apple's own privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Widgets and Live Activities (Lock Screen / Dynamic Island) read this same on-device data to display your timer and progress — nothing is sent externally to power them.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of its local data from your device. There is no account to delete and no server-side copy to remove, because none exists.

## Children's privacy

Weekly does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
